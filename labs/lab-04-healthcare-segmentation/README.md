# Lab 04 — Healthcare Network Segmentation

**Author:** [mari-2215](https://github.com/mari-2215)  
**Status:** Planned

## Objective

Segment clinical workstations, IoMT devices, administrative endpoints, servers, guests, and IT management. Apply least-privilege paths around an electronic health record service and demonstrate both privacy-oriented blocks and continuity scenarios.

## Planned evidence

- clinical-to-EHR permitted flow;
- guest-to-EHR denied flow;
- IoMT isolation;
- IT-only administration;
- service failure and recovery exercise;
- explicit statement of simulation and compliance limitations.
